# Support

Need a hand? Write to [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}). We usually answer within two days. You can also write from the app: **Settings > Contact support**.

## Common questions

### I didn't get the 6-digit code

Check your spam folder and wait a minute. Codes expire after 15 minutes. You can ask for a new one after 60 seconds.

### How do I share a list?

Create a free account first (guests can't share). Open the list, tap **Share**, type the person's @username or email, choose what they can do, and tap **Send invite**. They accept it from the **Inbox** (tray icon on the Lists tab). Invites by email don't send an email: the app lets you text them the link.

### Can I use it without an account?

Yes. Tap **Continue without an account**. Your lists are saved online under a random ID, so create an account later in **Settings** to keep them safe; everything comes with you.

### How do I delete my account and my data?

In the app: **Settings > Delete account** (or **Delete guest data**). It deletes your lists and their items (also for the people you shared them with), photos and invites right away. If you can't open the app, email us from the address on your account.

### Someone is bothering me with invites

In the **Inbox**, open the invite's menu and choose **Decline and block**. Their future invites are dropped silently. To report a list you're on, open it and tap **Share > Report this list**.

### I changed phones

Sign in with your email and password. Guest data can't move to a new phone; create an account on the old phone first.
